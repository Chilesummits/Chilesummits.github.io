abstract: >
  To evaluate the relevance of the Acoustic Adaptation Hypothesis in the territorial song
  of the Austral Thrush (*Turdus falcklandii*), we analysed six bioacoustic parameters in
  urban areas of Concepción, Chile, and assessed their association with disturbance
  variables (ambient noise, vegetation cover, built-up area, building height). Urban
  disturbance did not affect spectral parameters or song duration, but ambient noise
  significantly increased the temporal delivery rate of song elements, and ground cover
  type also influenced element rate.
summary: >
  Urban noise doesn't change the Austral Thrush's song frequency, but does make it sing
  faster.
