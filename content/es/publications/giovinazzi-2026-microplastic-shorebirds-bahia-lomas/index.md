abstract: >
  We used stable isotopes of carbon and nitrogen to investigate the diet of three
  migratory shorebirds - the Two-banded Plover (*Anarhynchus falklandicus*), Magellanic
  Oystercatcher (*Haematopus leucopodus*), and Hudsonian Godwit (*Limosa haemastica*) - at
  Bahía Lomas, Chile, and quantified microplastic ingestion. Microplastics were detected
  in sediments, macroinvertebrate prey, and fecal samples of all species, with
  contamination highest in Two-banded Plover, providing the first evidence of
  microplastic contamination in shorebirds and their trophic resources anywhere in Chile.
summary: >
  First evidence of microplastic contamination in Chilean shorebirds, highest in the
  Two-banded Plover at Bahía Lomas.
